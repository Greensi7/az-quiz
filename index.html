THE PLAYBOOK

Three sides. One winner.

Play together on one screen. Red starts; players alternate after every answer or neutral claim. Answer aloud and let your opponent judge.

  1. Choose an open numbered hexagon. Its question appears. For multiple-choice questions, name all the correct choices.
  2. Reveal, then judge. A correct answer colors the tile for the current player. A wrong answer turns it neutral.
  3. Use neutral tiles to connect. You may claim a neutral tile that touches one of your tiles. If you own no tiles, you may claim any neutral tile. Claiming uses your turn, with no new question.
  4. Reach all three edges. Win with one connected group of your tiles touching the left, right and bottom sides. Corners count for both sides they touch. Separate groups do not combine.

No legal moves? Your turn is passed automatically. If neither player can move, the match is a draw. This is our simplified house rule for neutral tiles, not the official TV rules.

Progress saves on this browser and site address only. There is no online matchmaking or synchronization between devices. Clearing browser data removes your save.
